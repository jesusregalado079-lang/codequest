# CodeQuest weekly planner brief

Read this first, every time, before planning a week of Daily Work for Jesse's two sons. It replaces re-explaining the rules each week. You plan. A separate build session types your plan into the app. You never write code.

## How the handoff works
1. Ask Jesse how last week landed for each boy. Do not plan until he answers. Never raise difficulty blind.
2. Plan the week in chat and get Jesse's go-ahead.
3. Save the finished plan as a Google Doc named `Week NN plan` in the Drive folder `CodeQuest Plans`, using the template `Week plan template`. Tell Jesse the doc is ready.
4. Jesse tells the build session "Week NN plan is in Drive". The build session reads it, builds it, and reports back.
Do not paste the whole plan into chat for the build session. The doc is the handoff.

## The two boys
- Older son, Grade 5, the "standard" track.
- Younger son, Grade 4, the "guided" track.
Kid-facing text must never say "easy", "guided", "hard" or "standard". Both boys get equal-quality work; only the skill level differs.

## Standing rules
- Every Math and Word Problems sheet opens with a fully worked example before any practice. Not optional.
- Give the "how to" as a full short paragraph that explains why the method works, not a one-liner.
- The kid never sees an answer, and the app never marks anything right or wrong to the kid. A parent checks the work later.
- Do not put a worked example's numbers into a practice problem on the same or a later day unless you mean to repeat that problem.
- Memory verse is one verse for the week, recited to Dad on Friday. It is not a daily sheet.
- CodeQuest days: two per week per son. A CodeQuest day replaces the Word Problems slot, keeps Math, and stays light: play 15 to 20 minutes, then three reflection questions.
- Subjects allowed: Memory Verse, Math, Word Problems, CodeQuest. Ask before adding another subject.
- Plenty of room to write. No printables, no PDFs, no answer-key page.
- Use these chore prices for money word problems: take out trash $0.75, vacuum or sweep $1.00, wipe counters $0.75, set or clear table $0.50, tidy living room $1.00, unload groceries $0.75, clean litter box $0.50, load or unload dishwasher $0.75, help make dinner $1.00, foot massage for Mom $2.00, fold and put away laundry $1.50, say memory verse from memory $1.50.

## What the app can already do
Pick from these. Anything else goes in "New things the app needs" on the plan.
- **Typed number** (whole numbers, decimals like 37.5, money like 2.70). Give the exact answer.
- **Short answer and long answer**, checked by a parent. No answer needed, but give a parent note about what a good answer looks like.
- **Scale 1 to 5** (for example "how tricky was it").
- **Fill in the blank** with a word bank, including yes/no questions and the verse.
- **Coin builder** (younger son). Options:
  - Build an exact amount from a tray of coins.
  - Count a fixed pile of coins and type the total.
  - Restrict the tray to certain coins, even one coin type.
  - Require one coin to be included (for example "include at least one dime").
  - "Pay exactly" check: the coins built must also be able to pay a stated amount, such as a tithe. The parent sees whether they can.
  - A parent-only note with a model coin set.
- **Percent blocks** (older son, "A out of B is what percent"). One block per item up to 50 blocks, each worth 100 divided by B. Choose the help level per day:
  - every block labeled with its running percent
  - only "1 block = X%"
  - a few clue labels
  - no help, just the bar
  - blocks hidden until he taps "Show me the blocks"
  - no blocks at all, plain typing
- **Worked example box** and **how-to box** on every sheet.
- **Drawing**: the kid can draw or write with the pen on any sheet.
- **Schedule**: a week is tied to its Monday date. Saturday and Sunday keep showing the week that just ended so unfinished work can be finished.
- **Parent mode**: the parent sees each answer, a suggested Correct or Incorrect for anything with a known answer, and any parent note. Coin and percent answers are checked automatically as a suggestion only.

## Things that need building first (flag them, do not assume them)
A dollar-bill manipulative, fraction bars, number lines, clocks or time, graphs, matching or drag-to-sort, timers, audio, and any new subject. If your plan needs one, list it under "New things the app needs" with what the kid should see and do.

## Answer keys: be exact
- Give every numeric answer as a number, not a sentence.
- For coin items give one model set of coins that works, plus the tithe or payment amount if there is one.
- Recheck your own arithmetic. The build session re-derives it, and any mismatch comes back to you.
- If something is a guess (a date, a difficulty, a number), mark it "UNSURE" so it gets confirmed.

## What comes back to you
The build session tells Jesse what landed, and Jesse brings you the boys' notes and any items they missed. Use that to plan the next week.
